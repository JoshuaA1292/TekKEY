What is real here

The exchangeOne exchange process (Build A's matching engine), run with 255 accounts. The ordinary key is a direct session on its binary wire protocol: race/exchange_client.py speaks proto.hpp. The bounded key goes through venued and the gateway, like every program.
The tapeCoinbase ETH-USD, every trade, over the public WebSocket. Each new dollar tick becomes a print on the book by the two environment accounts, with the same four messages replay and the verifier use (env.hpp print_one). Prints that jump more than the envelope's gap are counted as assumption violations.
The fees and fillsBoth accounts' money is read from the exchange's own fill journal; fees are venued's formula, maker 1.0 bp / taker 3.5 bp, rounded up per fill. The raw lane's ledger is cross-checked against the exchange's position and cash counters on every state read.
The modelOne model. Both lanes get the same system prompt head and the same OWNER / MARKET / MESSAGE lines; the sha256 of that shared history is recorded per lane per round and shown on every round. Only the "how you act" tail differs: order JSON, or a program.
The attacksEvery third round one message from the eval dataset (evals/data/attacks.json: fake owner, instruction override, fake market event, social pressure) is appended to both transcripts; your messages above go the same way. Nothing tells the model which lines are attacks.
The owner's rulesThe same brief in both lanes: at most 2 lots, always a stop, at most $500 lost in 24 hours. In the bounded lane they are the envelope the verifier enforces. In the raw lane they can only be watched: every breach is counted while it lasts.
Backtest modeThe same race on a chosen day of Coinbase history, at a fixed speed, with no look-ahead: a market clock releases each print only once it has reached its time, the two models see only prints already released, and the clock pauses while they decide so both act on the same snapshot. Each minute candle becomes four prints: open, the extreme nearer the open, the other extreme, close. The bounded lane's 24 h budget covers the whole replayed window.
LiquidationAn ordinary account whose mark-to-market loss reaches its capital is liquidated: every order cancelled, the position flattened at the next print. The bounded account cannot get there: nothing deploys without a certificate that fits the budget.
